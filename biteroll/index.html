开始录像－下一竿会不一样

这是一款为钓鱼场景设计的 iPhone 预录相机。

如何录制

允许相机和麦克风访问，保持应用在前台。选择 15、30 或 60 秒预录窗口，并等待缓存填充。按下录像按钮后，应用会保留窗口内已经缓存的画面并继续录像。停止后，在片段库播放、分享或保存到相册。刚开始预录时，缓存可能短于所选窗口。

边框代表什么

  • 青蓝流动：预录窗口正在填充。
  • 稳定亮绿：预录窗口就绪。
  • 红色呼吸：正在录像。
  • 黄色过渡：正在整理保存。

语音和水平处理

中文语音口令为实验性功能，需要识别授权及设备本地识别支持。目前没有英文语音口令。录后水平处理也是实验性功能，保留原片，需结束预录并保持前台和适宜设备温度。

恢复失败怎么办

不要卸载应用或删除应用文稿后再反馈，这可能移除尚未恢复的原始分段。记录应用版本、出现错误的步骤和错误文字。应用可能保留诊断分段；只在自己愿意且确认接收方后分享这些文件，其中可能包含声音、视频和地点。

权限和地点

可在 iPhone 系统设置中调整相机、麦克风、语音识别、定位及相册添加权限。授权定位后，视频及其导出副本可能包含拍摄地点；分享前请留意这一点。

Support page (English)

BiteRoll: Fishing Camera

BiteRoll is an iPhone pre-recording camera designed for fishing.

Grant camera and microphone access, keep the app in the foreground, select a 15-, 30-, or 60-second buffer, and let it fill. Starting a recording includes footage already available in that buffer. Stop the recording to replay, share, or save the clip to Photos. A newly started buffer can contain less footage than the chosen duration.

The border flows cyan-blue while the buffer fills, becomes steady green when ready, pulses red during recording, and turns yellow while saving.

Chinese voice commands and post-recording horizon processing are experimental. Voice commands require permission and on-device Chinese recognition support; English commands are not supported. Horizon processing preserves the original and requires stopping pre-recording, foreground operation, and suitable temperature.

If recovery fails, avoid uninstalling the app or deleting its documents before you report the issue. Those actions can remove retained fragments. Share your app version, device, iOS version, steps, and error text. Diagnostic fragments can contain video, audio, and location; share them only deliberately with a recipient you trust.

Location permission can add the filming location to video metadata and exported copies. Review this before sharing.
